I am an undergraduate Biology student at the University of California, Riverside, with a strong interest in ecology, microbiology, and the ways scientific research can improve our understanding of the natural world. My academic experience has given me a foundation in biology, laboratory methods, scientific communication, and careful observation. I enjoy connecting what I learn in the classroom with practical research and community-focused projects.

Through my research experience in the Anderson Lab, I have developed skills in maintaining organized records, following laboratory protocols, observing biological samples, and communicating scientific information clearly. Laboratory coursework has also helped me build experience with microbiology techniques, microscopy, bacterial culture, antibiotic testing, DNA analysis, and quantitative measurements. These experiences have strengthened my attention to detail and taught me the importance of accuracy, patience, and consistency in scientific work.

I am also helping establish ESA SEEDS at UCR, an organization focused on Strategies for Ecology Education, Diversity, and Sustainability. This work has allowed me to practice leadership, organization, outreach, and collaborative planning. I hope to help create opportunities for students to learn about ecology, participate in research, develop professional connections, and feel supported in pursuing careers in environmental and biological sciences. My long-term goal is to continue developing as a scientist while contributing to research, education, and inclusive environmental leadership.
